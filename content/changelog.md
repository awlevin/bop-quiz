# What's new in Bop Quiz

Notes on what's changed. Newest first.

## November 24, 2026

### 🔥 New Features
- **🧠 Smart setlist (experimental).** A new setlist mode that reads the room. It opens with a song everyone knows, follows a stumper with an easier one, reaches for deeper cuts when you're on a roll, and slips in the odd surprise banger. It remembers your room from game to game. Preview it on [/shuffle](/shuffle).

## November 23, 2026

### 🔥 New Features
- **🎚️ Easy, Medium or Hard (experimental).** A new *Difficulty* setlist mode plays only the best-known songs of every decade since the '60s: the top 10 of each on Easy, 20 on Medium, 30 on Hard, or pick your own number.
- **🔢 Shape the game (experimental).** The host's settings menu has *Rounds*: choose how many songs ask for the title, the artist, the year and the album, and play each clip up to three times. It works with every setlist mode.
- **🏳️ Give-ups on every phone.** When someone gives up, the other phones show a small "Ben gave up" note for a moment. The host can keep it to the TV instead.
- **🤷 Giving up is a bet.** Tapping out calls a stumper: if nobody still guessing gets it, you score 300. Then heckle the big screen from your phone with tomatoes, air horns and slow claps until the song ends.
- **🤝 Ride along (house rule).** Turn it on in the host's settings and giving up can back a player who's still in instead, for a quarter of whatever they score. Off by default.

### 💅 Nits
- **What's new has a home.** This page. Find it from the link at the bottom of the home screen.

## November 22, 2026

### 🔥 New Features
- **🎛️ Pick the setlist (experimental).** The host's settings menu has a Setlist mode. *Crowd picks* lets every phone choose genres and decades in the lobby and mixes everyone's picks fairly, so the lone soul fan still gets their songs. *Host picks* lets the TV choose for the room. *Classic* is the whole catalog, as before. A pick too narrow for a whole game is topped up with the closest songs.
- **End a game from the pause screen.** The host, on the TV or the first player's phone, can end a paused game. Everyone goes back to the lobby with the scores cleared, ready for another round.

### 🐛 Bug fixes
- **Songs you actually know, again.** The big catalog had too many deep cuts, so we're back to the 200 crowd-pleasers. The fixes for songs that used to play the wrong track stay.

### 💅 Nits
- **A sound check before the first game.** The TV plays a short chime and someone at the screen confirms they heard it, so no game starts in silence. Run it again any time from the settings menu in the lobby.
- **Giving up is quiet now.** The white flag still drops on the TV. The sad trombone doesn't play anymore.
- **Album rounds are a fair draw again.** They come from the game's own setlist, as they did at launch, instead of from artists the room had already shown it knew.
- **Try the setlist modes on [/shuffle](/shuffle).** Redraw a game as Classic, Crowd picks, or Host picks, with a few made-up picks, before you try one for real.

## October 8, 2026

### 🔥 New Features
- **🎧 Quick play.** Nobody around? Play ten songs on one screen: no room, no phones. The reveal comes as soon as you lock in, the final screen lists every song with what you typed, and your best score is saved in that browser. Reload mid-song and you pick up where you were.

### 💅 Nits
- **We're Bop Quiz now.** New name, new address: bop-quiz.vercel.app. Your saved name and settings came with you.
- **Album rounds you have a shot at.** The two album rounds pick artists the room got right earlier in the game, and the second goes to whoever the first one didn't help.

## September 21, 2026

### 💅 Nits
- **Fades you can hear.** Songs fade out over the end of the reveal instead of stopping abruptly, and the fades sound smooth all the way through. A song that already faded stays gone after a pause.

## September 19, 2026

### 🔥 New Features
- **⏸️ Pause.** Any phone can pause the game. The clocks stop, you get your time back when you resume, and the TV plays an intermission with liner notes over the crackle of a run-out groove.
- **📮 Feedback board.** Send feedback from your phone or the TV's gear menu, or flag a song that's off right from the reveal. Every report gets an id and a public status on the [feedback board](/feedback), and a flagged song sits out until it's sorted.
- **A much bigger catalog.** 1,174 songs, from the 1960s on.

### 🐛 Bug fixes
- **The right song plays.** Eleven songs used to play a different track from the same album (Faith played Father Figure). Fixed.

### 💅 Nits
- **Crossfades everywhere.** Every change of song crossfades, the needle swings in, and the record winds up and coasts down instead of snapping.
- **Fairer year scoring.** Year answers score on a curve: one year off still earns 92%, three off about half, and seven or more off earns nothing.
- **The answer, big.** The reveal on your phone leads with the answer the round asked for, in large type.
- **Fresh songs all night.** The TV remembers what it has played, so a second game doesn't repeat songs from the first.
- **The final scores get a playlist.** Party songs play into each other while the scores sink in.
- **Less on screen.** The settings gear shows only in the lobby and on the final screen, *Send feedback* only at the reveal, and the wordmark takes you home.
- **Looks good when shared.** A proper icon, a card for links shared in chats, and Bop Quiz can go on your phone's home screen.
- **Peek at the draw.** [/shuffle](/shuffle) deals out game after game from the catalog, the way a real room would.

## September 18, 2026

### 🔥 New Features
- **🎶 The game is live.** One screen plays the song, and up to twelve players race to name it from their phones. Ten rounds ask for the title, artist, year, and album. Faster answers score more, typos are forgiven, and the album sleeve slides out at the reveal.
- **Your phone runs the show.** The first player in starts the game from their phone, so nobody has to get up and go to the TV.
- **Give up.** Tap out of a round you'll never get, and a white flag drops on the TV.

### 💅 Nits
- **A heads-up when the question changes.** A full-screen card announces each new kind of question, on the TV and on every phone.
- **Lobby music.** A song plays quietly while people join. Turn it off from the gear menu on the TV.
- **A song to see you out.** The final scores play a party song, never one the game just asked about.
