# What's new in Bop Quiz

Notes on what's changed. Newest first.

## November 23, 2026

### 💅 Nits
- **What's new has a home.** This page. Find it from the link at the bottom of the home screen.

## November 22, 2026

### 🔥 New Features
- **🎛️ Pick the setlist (experimental).** The host's settings menu has a Setlist mode. *Crowd picks* lets every phone choose genres and decades in the lobby and mixes everyone's picks fairly, so the lone soul fan still gets their songs. *Host picks* lets the TV choose for the room. *Classic* is the whole catalog, as before. [/shuffle](/shuffle) previews each mode.
- **End a game from the pause screen.** The host, on the TV or the first player's phone, can end a paused game. Everyone goes back to the lobby with the scores cleared, ready for another round.

### 🐛 Bug fixes
- **Songs you actually know, again.** The big catalog had too many deep cuts, so we're back to the 200 crowd-pleasers. The fixes for songs that used to play the wrong track stay.

### 💅 Nits
- **A sound check before the first game.** The TV plays a short chime and someone at the screen confirms they heard it, so no game starts in silence.
- **Giving up is quiet now.** The white flag still drops on the TV. The sad trombone doesn't play anymore.

## October 8, 2026

### 🔥 New Features
- **🎧 Quick play.** Nobody around? Play ten songs on one screen: no room, no phones. The reveal comes as soon as you lock in, and your best score is saved in that browser.

### 💅 Nits
- **We're Bop Quiz now.** New name, new address: bop-quiz.vercel.app. Your saved name and settings came with you.

## September 21, 2026

### 💅 Nits
- **Fades you can hear.** Songs fade out over the end of the reveal instead of stopping abruptly, and the fades sound smooth all the way through.

## September 19, 2026

### 🔥 New Features
- **⏸️ Pause.** Any phone can pause the game. The clocks stop, you get your time back when you resume, and the TV plays an intermission with liner notes.
- **📮 Feedback board.** Send feedback from your phone or the TV's gear menu. Every report gets an id and a public status on the [feedback board](/feedback), and a song reported as off sits out until it's sorted.
- **A much bigger catalog.** 1,174 songs, from the 1960s on.

### 🐛 Bug fixes
- **The right song plays.** Eleven songs used to play a different track from the same album (Faith played Father Figure). Fixed.

### 💅 Nits
- **Crossfades everywhere.** Every change of song crossfades, the needle swings in, and the record winds up and coasts down instead of snapping.
- **Fairer year scoring.** Year answers score on a curve: one year off still earns 92%, three off about half, and seven or more off earns nothing.
- **The answer, big.** The reveal on your phone leads with the answer the round asked for, in large type.
- **Fresh songs all night.** The TV remembers what it has played, so a second game doesn't repeat songs from the first.
- **The final scores get a playlist.** Party songs play into each other while the scores sink in.

## September 18, 2026

### 🔥 New Features
- **🎶 The game is live.** One screen plays the song, and everyone races to name it from their phone. Ten rounds ask for the title, artist, year, and album. Faster answers score more, and typos are forgiven.
- **Your phone runs the show.** The first player in starts the game from their phone, so nobody has to get up and go to the TV.
- **Give up.** Tap out of a round you'll never get, and a white flag drops on the TV.

### 💅 Nits
- **A heads-up when the question changes.** A full-screen card announces each new kind of question, on the TV and on every phone.
- **Lobby music.** A song plays quietly while people join. Turn it off from the gear menu on the TV.
